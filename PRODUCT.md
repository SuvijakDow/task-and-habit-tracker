# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single primary user, a university student using this app daily for personal productivity. The user quickly checks off completed daily habits, monitors consistency and streak trends in analytics, and adds university work with due dates under appropriate categories.

## Product Purpose

This product is a fast personal task and habit companion that keeps daily planning lightweight. It exists to reduce mental overhead, support routine consistency, and prevent missed deadlines. Success means the user can capture and update habits or tasks in seconds, with smooth performance and no friction.

## Brand Personality

Beautiful, convenient, fast. The tone should feel calm, focused, and practical, helping the user stay in flow without visual noise or unnecessary complexity.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered dashboard interfaces, crowded card stacks, and decorative patterns that slow interaction. Avoid heavy or distracting motion that competes with the user's core actions.

## Design Principles

1. Finish key actions in seconds.
2. Prioritize consistency tracking over visual novelty.
3. Keep information clear, compact, and scannable.
4. Use visuals to support speed, not to add friction.
5. Make everyday usage feel stable, smooth, and predictable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and readability. Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep animations optional and non-essential. Ensure clear keyboard focus states, readable text sizing, and color usage that does not rely on hue alone.
